Scene notes

  • Coverage stays on the south side of the counter so Arjun always looks screen left and Lakshmi screen right. Lakshmi gets closer framings as she softens (MS, MCU, then CU); the scene opens and closes on the same wide.

Script

  1. Rain hammers a tin roof. The platform is empty except for a chai stall with one bulb still burning.
  2. LAKSHMI (60s), the stall owner, wipes down glasses she has already wiped twice.
  3. ARJUN (20s), soaked, a guitar case on his back, runs in and stops under the roof.
  4. ARJUN The 11:40 to Hyderabad?
  5. LAKSHMI Left at 11:40.
  6. Arjun looks at the empty track. He sets the guitar case down and sits on it.
  7. LAKSHMI Next one is at five. Chai?
  8. ARJUN I've got twelve rupees.
  9. She is already pouring.
  10. LAKSHMI Then it's a twelve-rupee chai.
  11. He laughs for the first time. The bulb flickers.
